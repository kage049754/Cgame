# Third-party assets and licenses

The first playable prototype uses procedural meshes, built-in fonts, and code-generated card labels. No external art, model, music, or sound asset is currently bundled.

Before adding third-party assets, record:
- Asset name and creator
- Original source URL
- Exact license (including version)
- Attribution text, if required
- Modifications made

Prefer CC0 or permissively licensed assets. Never assume that a free download permits commercial use.
