# Third-party assets and licenses

## Imported assets

- **Quaternius Universal Base Characters — Superhero Male (Standard)**: `assets/characters/quaternius_superhero_male.glb`. The 741 KB GLB is a modified derivative of the official Standard model: it retains the source mesh, humanoid skeleton, and skin weights while using a matte material and omitting image payloads. It is used as the dealer character silhouette. The upstream source page identifies the Standard pack as CC0 1.0 Universal. The original license notice is preserved at `assets/licenses/QUATERNIUS-CC0-1.0.txt`.
  - Official pack: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
  - Free Standard download: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
  - License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  - Source asset: `Base Characters/Godot - UE/Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf`
  - Upstream preparation notes and derivative provenance: https://github.com/programasweights/avatar/blob/main/ASSETS.md

## Additional Quaternius candidates

- **Zombie Apocalypse Kit** — 4 playable characters, 4 enemies, 2 dogs, and environmental/vehicle models. The official pack page identifies it as CC0 and allows commercial use. https://quaternius.com/packs/zombieapocalypsekit.html
- **Universal Animation Library** — 120+ retargetable humanoid animations for Godot, Unity, and Unreal. The official page identifies it as CC0 and allows commercial use. https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

Quaternius' site-wide Quaternius Asset License (QAL) v1.0, updated 2026-08-28, also permits commercial incorporation without attribution but prohibits redistributing assets as standalone asset packs. The specific Standard model used above carries CC0 1.0 Universal; keep its notice and provenance with the project.

## Integration rules

Record the exact pack/version, source page, license, date obtained, and modifications for every imported asset. Prefer glTF/GLB. Do not commit paid-only Source-tier files or redistribute asset packs separately. Keep assets limited to models actually used by the game.
